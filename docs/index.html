ABOUT

Building connections across the energy supply chain.

Saleh Energy Trading (SET) is a Bangladesh-based energy trading and commercial facilitation company focused on connecting international energy suppliers with established buyers in high-growth markets.

Operating from Dhaka, SET develops relationships across the energy supply chain and works closely with suppliers, traders, importers and industrial partners. Our business is built around understanding both sides of a transaction — the requirements of international suppliers and the commercial and operational needs of buyers.

We help bridge that gap through market access, sourcing, commercial coordination and long-term relationship development.

Current focus

Our initial focus is the bulk LPG market, with the objective of expanding our activities across the wider energy and commodity sector as the company grows.

WHAT WE DO

Where we sit in the transaction.

We facilitate energy trading opportunities by working closely with both sides of every transaction, from first introduction through to delivery.

Energy trading & sourcing

SET identifies international supply opportunities and connects them with qualified buyers in our target markets. We work to understand specifications, quantities, delivery requirements and commercial structures before bringing counterparties together.

Buyer & supplier facilitation

Strong transactions begin with the right counterparties. SET develops relationships with both international suppliers and regional buyers, helping facilitate introductions, negotiations and ongoing commercial communication.

Market development

We assist international suppliers seeking access to the Bangladesh market by providing local market knowledge, commercial relationships and business-development support, while helping buyers identify potential international sources of energy products.

Commercial coordination

SET assists counterparties throughout commercial discussions, including coordination surrounding:

  • Product specifications & volumes
  • Pricing & payment structures
  • Delivery terms & inspection
  • Documentation & contract discussions

Logistics coordination

Energy transactions require coordination between suppliers, buyers, vessels, ports and inspection companies. SET helps facilitate communication regarding delivery structures, vessel requirements, discharge planning and other logistical considerations.

Long-term supply relationships

Our objective extends beyond individual transactions. SET seeks to develop lasting relationships between reliable suppliers and established buyers, creating opportunities for recurring supply programs.

PRODUCTS

Liquefied Petroleum Gas (LPG)

Bulk LPG is currently a primary focus of Saleh Energy Trading.

SET works to connect international LPG supply with established importers and buyers within Bangladesh and other regional markets. Depending on buyer requirements and supplier availability, commercial discussions may cover different propane and butane compositions, cargo quantities and delivery structures.

Final contractual terms remain subject to agreement between the relevant counterparties.
